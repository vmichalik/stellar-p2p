Release transaction: `7acb287096e02b15c80b4c18b0485612efed95fd7b5e83c5e5b1be11cbe1ae5d`. Mutual half-split recovery transaction: `866f81532c6cc42c21e9a98f751440c4917b77ee3aad97a7eb81774e41a85165`. These are public testnet records; they contain no bank payment data.

The first recovery attempt exposed an authorization-entry serialization bug. The successful run used rebuilt XDR-backed operations after that fix. The run validates the reviewed contract and the selected host/adapter path with synthetic terms and disposable keys; it is not evidence of live wallet UI or bank integration.

## Remaining gate

The normalizer in `policy/src/evidence.mjs` deliberately throws until the proof owner establishes the real response schema, transfer association, source/account and recipient identities, amount units/sign, outgoing/completed state, timestamp, and stable transfer identifier. Keep the gate closed until those semantics are backed by accepted verifier evidence and negative cases are tested. No real USDC or bank-value test is authorized or represented here.

Other limits include production key custody and operational deployment, Freighter UI behavior, and a release-built/runtime-tested verifier privacy patch. For focused explanations see the [architecture study](architecture/study.html), [readiness guide](architecture/operations-and-readiness.md), and [integration setup](../integration/README.md). Exact checks belong in the [verification record](verification.md); do not infer current totals from older snapshots.
