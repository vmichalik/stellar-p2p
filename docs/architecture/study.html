Two checks connect the trade.

TLSNotary authenticates the bank response. Pactlane’s payment service checks who paid whom, the exact amount, timing and payment identity. The contract then verifies the service’s signature and its own stored trade rules.

Read about claims and replay protection →

Recovery needs both wallets.

If a payment check fails after locking, the tokens stay held. A later valid proof can complete the trade, or both wallets can approve a release, refund or split. Neither participant can take a unilateral timeout refund after the lock.
