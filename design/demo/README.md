# Pactlane film

The 90-second composition uses fresh captures of the integrated app in Demo mode. The evidence card cites the accepted bank record and the separate PLTEST testnet release/recovery. The layout, local Manrope font and restrained transitions match the website.

Open `index.html` through a local HTTP preview. Its paused GSAP timeline is exposed as `window.__timelines.pactlane` for deterministic seeking. `hyperframes.json` retains the composition's project settings. Render at 1920×1080, 30fps without audio.

Replace product screenshots using the [shot list](../../docs/demo/shot-list.md). A real mainnet settlement scene needs its matching order and transaction receipt. Keep bank details out of screenshots and Git.

Manrope is bundled with its OFL notice. The unchanged GSAP runtime retains its licence header. The source capture and render checks are linked in [verification](../../docs/verification.md).
