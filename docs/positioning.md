# Pactlane: the user problem and product promise

Researched 25 October 2026. This is the coordinator's current product direction, based on official product/support sources and the implemented prototype. It supersedes a generic low-fee or privacy-first pitch. It is desk research, not customer validation.

## Decision

Lead with the gap between making a bank payment and receiving crypto: **“I've sent the euros. What has to happen before I get my USDC?”**

The initial audience is an existing Stellar wallet user with EUR in Revolut who already uses, or would consider, P2P liquidity. Start with a small Stellar-community liquidity cohort. The seller is equally important: they need an exact payment match before releasing their USDC. This is an early-adopter hypothesis, not proof of a market.

Pactlane is being built to connect a funded offer, a payment check and a clear release decision in one trade. The intended improvement is less reliance on messages and buyer-supplied receipts, with an explicit current actor and next action. Actual reductions in waiting, disputes or cost have not been measured.

**Headline:** Trade euros for USDC on Stellar.

**Supporting line:** A clearer handoff from Revolut payment to USDC release.

**Problem prompt:** After you pay, know what happens next.

## Put the promise into the experience

| Surface              | Copy and behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Entry                | Use the headline and supporting line above. Keep a concise “Simulated walkthrough · No live payment or settlement” badge visible.                                                                                         |
| Quote                | “You pay €[amount]. You receive [exact amount] USDC.” Whole-offer acceptance. Label fixture pricing “Demo fee”; do not imply future zero pricing. Preserve required token precision.                                      |
| Before payment       | “Confirm the payment lock before sending euros.” Recipient instructions appear only after confirmed lock. Explain: “After locking, USDC stays held until payment is accepted or both wallets approve recovery.”           |
| Payment deadline     | Say “Send payment within …”. It is not a guarantee that proof finishes before that time. A timely payment may be verified later.                                                                                          |
| Progress             | State who acts next, what has been confirmed, and what remains. A spinner or browser callback is not payment acceptance.                                                                                                  |
| Consent              | “Review the information requested for this payment check.” Name the actual verifier destination and selected fields. In the demo, clearly identify sample data.                                                           |
| Verification failure | “Payment verification incomplete. USDC remains held. Retry verification or agree a recovery with the seller.” Keep “Don't pay again” when a payment may already exist.                                                    |
| Recovery             | “Recovery requires approval from both parties. There is no automatic refund after payment begins.” Show exact allocations, approvals still needed and held amount. An uncooperative party can leave the trade unresolved. |
| Receipt              | Show the exact amounts and actual evidence type. A demo receipt is not a chain explorer transaction. Use “View receipt details” for an internal modal.                                                                    |
| Trust explanation    | “Pactlane's verifier checks payment evidence and authorizes release. The Stellar contract checks that authorization.” The contract does not directly validate a bank transcript.                                          |

## Pitch and demo

Opening narration: “In a manual P2P trade, sending euros and receiving crypto are two separate steps. If confirmation stalls, the trade can turn into messages, receipts and a support case. Pactlane explores a clearer flow for Revolut users who want USDC in a Stellar wallet: fund the offer, lock the trade, check the payment, then release.”

The main story follows seller funding, buyer whole-offer reservation, confirmed payment locking, then fiat instructions, sample payment verification and a simulated receipt. A separate order demonstrates unavailable proof and two-wallet recovery. The local policy/compiled-WASM rehearsal is distinct implementation evidence. Do not intercut it as if it proves the browser made a live payment.

Closing narration: “This walkthrough uses simulated payment verification. The next milestone is reliable acceptance of a real Revolut payment. If a payment cannot be verified, funds remain held until valid proof arrives or both parties authorize recovery.”

The new public demo address is `https://pactlane.io`. Use `https://proof.pactlane.io` for proof tooling only after DNS, certificate and public-origin configuration are verified. Domain ownership does not establish service availability. Keep video audio absent for Vijay's own dub.

## What could invalidate the idea

The extension, proof generation, fixed offer sizes, unfamiliar verifier and two-party recovery may introduce more friction than they remove. Reliable liquidity and seller risk are prerequisites. Cryptographic evidence of a payment record does not make the underlying payment irreversible or eliminate chargebacks, account freezes and every dispute. The current prototype has not demonstrated accepted live payment semantics or fewer disputes.

Interview buyers and liquidity providers separately. Ask about their last actual EUR-to-Stellar trade: why Stellar, final amount received, all-in costs, steps and any delay. Ask for an example before suggesting our solution. Then test whether they understand the payment lock, proof request and recovery dependency. Compare task completion and support interventions against their existing route. Do not count interest in a demo as willingness to trade or supply liquidity.

Working confidence: high that payment-to-release coordination failures exist; medium that authenticated payment checks can help; low that this specific audience will choose Pactlane until direct research and a reliable pilot establish it.
